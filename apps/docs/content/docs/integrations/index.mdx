---
title: Integrations
description: Let your agent discover and load remotely served skills.
---

Connect your agent to published skills through its framework's native skill tools.
The agent sees a catalog of names and descriptions, then loads instructions and
supporting files when they fit the task.

- [Vercel AI SDK](/docs/vercel-ai-sdk): connect an AI SDK agent to remote skills.
- [LangChain and DeepAgents](/docs/integrations/langchain): connect a LangChain,
  DeepAgents, or LangGraph agent in TypeScript or Python.

- [Mastra](/docs/integrations/mastra): use Mastra's native Workspace tools to load, read, and search remote skills.

[Use the SDK](/docs/consume) to build your own integration. All integrations share
its [trust boundary](/docs/trust-and-security): loading a skill does not grant tool
permissions or execute its scripts.
